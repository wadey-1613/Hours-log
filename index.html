Payslip

This is an estimate, not a payslip. Weeks belong to the month their Friday falls in. The last week of the month is paid up to your cap and any hours over it are paid the next month at the same rate. Tax is worked out month by month, so your real payslip can differ by a few pounds. Bonuses and one-off deductions go in Extras for the month. Student loans are not included.

Used to work out when to leave for each job. The drive time comes from free map services (postcodes.io and OSRM), so your home postcode and each job's postcode are sent to them. Send from Gmail

Needed once so the app can send email from your Gmail. Until you add it, use Share to Mail or Outlook when you send.

How to get the code (on a computer)
  1. Go to console.cloud.google.com and sign in with your Gmail. Create a project called Hours Log.
  2. Open APIs and Services, then Library. Search for Gmail API and press Enable.
  3. Open OAuth consent screen. Choose External, fill in the app name and your email, and under Test users add your own Gmail address.
  4. Open Credentials, then Create credentials, then OAuth client ID. Choose Web application.
  5. Under Authorised JavaScript origins add https://wadey-1613.github.io
  6. Press Create, copy the Client ID and paste it above.

The first time you send, Google says the app is not verified. That is normal for your own app: press Advanced, then continue.

Back up your hours

Your hours live on this phone. A backup saves your days, schedule, pay settings and payslips to a file you keep (save it to Files or iCloud Drive). Do this every few weeks. Restoring puts those days back; days in the file replace the same days here and all other days stay.

Import schedule

First time? Add the Gmail read permission
  1. On a computer, open console.cloud.google.com and choose your Hours Log project.
  2. Open Google Auth Platform, then Data Access, then Add or remove scopes.
  3. Find the Gmail API scope that ends in gmail.readonly, tick it and Save.
  4. Come back here and press the button. Google will ask you to approve reading your mail. Press Advanced, then continue, as before.

The app only fetches emails from the sender you enter, and keeps nothing from Google except what you save here. The permission itself covers your whole mailbox, because Google has no narrower one.

Or copy the schedule email and press Import schedule. It is picked up from your clipboard automatically. If nothing appears, press Paste from clipboard, or paste it into the box below.

Importing replaces any schedule already saved for the same dates.
